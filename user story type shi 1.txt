Five most important user stories

1. As a Maths teacher, I need a way to for my students to practice their maths problems because there are not enough decent usbable options.
2. As a teacher, I need my students to study outside of class time so that they could study more effectively.
3. As a student, I need a way to work through my difficult problems step by step so that I can learn more effectively.
4. As a teacher, I need a way to easily add new parts of work so that curriculums can change over time.
5. As a teacher, I need a way to support my students with learning disabilities such as dyslexia so that they can partake in learning.
